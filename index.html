What is NEOTech?

NEOTech 2027 is an Educational Technology Conference for educators and school administrators in Northeast Ohio. NEOTech aims to provide attendees with valuable insights and tools to enhance their teaching methods and overall educational experience. Participants can look forward to engaging sessions and networking opportunities to stay updated with the evolving landscape of educational technology.

Innovative Sessions

Over 50 breakout sessions covering AI in education, Google tools, digital citizenship, project-based learning, and much more.

Networking

Connect with hundreds of like-minded educators. Share challenges, brainstorm solutions, and build a supportive professional learning network.

Sponsor Hall

Explore the newest hardware, software, and services from leading EdTech companies and local organizations. Get hands-on demos and find solutions for your school.

NEOTech Robotics Competition

Join us for the annual NEOTech Middle School Robotics Competition featuring several exciting games. Is your school ready to compete?
