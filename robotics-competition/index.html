Robotics Competition

Each year, NEOTech hosts a robotics competition for local middle school students. Participants use the NRC's guidelines to compete in tournaments in Boxbot, Mini Sumo, and Maze.

WHAT?

Robotics Competition for local 5th-8th Grade Middle School Students.

Students' families, teachers, and administrators are welcome to spectate.

WHERE?

Kent State University
Student Center Ballroom

1075 Risman Dr, Kent, OH 44242

WHEN?

March 16th, 2027

Competitions begin at 9:30am
